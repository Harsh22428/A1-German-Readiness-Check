Use your real experience for the introduction. Do not read invented credentials or claim that planned research has happened.

| Time | Show and explain |
|---|---|

Prepare an uneven example: vocabulary 5/5, grammar 5/5, reading 2/5. Its 80% total does not override the reading gap. For a real demonstration, answer through the app; do not present an illustrative score as a learner’s outcome.

Before sharing, check the video is one recording, 5–7 minutes, audible, readable and accessible from a signed-out browser. The app cannot record your personal introduction for you.
