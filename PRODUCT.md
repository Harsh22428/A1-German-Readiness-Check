# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Adults who have completed A1 German and are considering a Goethe or ÖSD exam. They need an understandable check of vocabulary, grammar and reading, a clear next action, and visibility of the skills not assessed. Mobile use and interrupted sessions are hypotheses from the approved PRD, not established research findings.

## Product Purpose

Implement the approved ../PRD.md: fifteen questions, transparent foundation bands, two or three response-linked recommendations, answer review and full-mock resources. Strong foundations means take a full mock next, never guaranteed exam readiness.

## Brand Personality

Supportive, clear, trustworthy. Use plain English around German questions. The candidate prototype is independent and must not impersonate Globio or exam providers.

## Anti-references

Avoid shame, certificates implying a pass, gamified streaks, pressured booking flows, inflated success statistics, decorative dashboards, and hidden assessment limitations. The PRD specifically rejects these behaviours.

## Design Principles

1. Explain the evidence and its limits.
2. Keep one task prominent at a time.
3. Translate mistakes into practical next steps.
4. Protect interrupted work and explain failures.
5. Familiar accessible controls over novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA target. Keyboard and screen-reader support, visible focus, 320px reflow, enlarged text, reduced motion, non-colour state labels and comfortable touch targets. English instructions with German text language attributes. No microphone or camera needed.
